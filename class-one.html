Road to Damascus — Class One

Class One.
The first.

Suwannee Correctional Institution, Florida

A word from Frank

They finished.

This is the first class that walked the Road to Damascus with us at Suwannee and finished.

We are not listing their names. The same way we honored Class Two. They are brothers. They are family. A certificate is not the end of the story. It is the proof that sharing a life with a few is how disciples are made.

Class Two came after them. The door did not close.

— Frank Ortega

With gratitude

To the staff who stood in the room.

None of this happens without the leaders who open the door and stay in the room when a class finishes.

The road continued

Class Two walked it next.
