01 — Why we exist

One reason: to obey Jesus by loving and serving the least of these.

Scripture makes it clear—when we feed the hungry, care for the hurting, visit the prisoner, and serve the overlooked, we're not just helping people… we're ministering to the heart of God. Jesus said what we do for "the least of these," we do for Him.

That's why we go to the uncelebrated places.

  • The forgotten places.
  • The places where nobody's clapping.
  • The places where people can't give us anything back.

Because we have freely received, we freely give. This is pure ministry: clean hands and a pure heart—serving practically and pouring out spiritually. Bringing real love, real truth, real prayer, and the real Gospel to those who need it most.

If God has put that same conviction in you, we invite you to get involved. Come serve with us as we take light into dark places and love the least of these—like Jesus does.
