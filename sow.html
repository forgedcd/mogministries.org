02 — Sowing Books

Put something in someone's hands

This is for the person who's like, "I don't just want to give. I want to KNOW someone is holding what I sow."

When you sow books, you're funding discipleship resources that go directly into people's hands for free, especially behind the walls.

Here's the simple math:

$11 = 1 book sown.

That $11 covers printing the book AND getting it distributed.

We set it up this way because it's about half the Amazon retail pricing, and it lets you sow something tangible into someone's life without wondering where it went.

03 — General Sowing

Fuel the mission wherever it's needed most

General sowing is for the person who simply wants to say, "Put my seed wherever the need is greatest right now."

This helps us stay flexible and respond in real time to what's in front of us.

This is the kind of giving that keeps the whole engine running.

04 — What your giving does

Whether you partner, sow books, or give generally, your giving helps us:

  • Bring hope to inmates behind the walls
  • Put discipleship resources into people's hands for free
  • Reach the youth of this generation with engaging, Scripture-rooted content
  • Fund audio books and free discipleship content behind the walls
  • Sow back into the places and organizations we minister with
